# HERMES → FALCON Contract

## Authority

HERMES is market-truth authority. FALCON receives bounded
decision-relevant facts/state and provenance, not HERMES's complete
historical/raw market corpus.

## Evidence

HERMES may publish registered: - market facts; - market state; -
quality/freshness transitions; - health relevant to evidence usability.

A market fact must preserve deterministic identity,
derivation/configuration identity, instrument/contract/series semantics,
observation/as-of/availability timing, quality, value/unit/reference
price and source/provenance reference.

## Temporal requirement

`available_at_utc` participates in causal eligibility. A retrospective
measurement must never appear as contemporaneously knowable evidence.

## Historical boundary

Quantitative historical/backtesting authority remains HERMES. FALCON is
the empirical operational/trading evidence corpus.

## Failure

Missing/stale HERMES evidence fails loudly according to consumer policy.
FALCON must never infer neutral market state from source failure.
